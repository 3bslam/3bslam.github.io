Scoop Snake: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Scoop Snake (package com.scoopsnake.game), published by Ayman Mohamed Abd Elsalam ("we"), handles and why.

1. Data the game itself keeps

Scoop Snake has no accounts, logins or servers of its own. Your progress and settings (unlocked heroes, the current run, options) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID and device identifiersShowing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate location (derived from IP address)Ad relevance, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads, app and device information, crash and diagnostic dataAd delivery, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch you are asked whether ads may be personalised. You can change this later from the game's options or by reinstalling the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • No ads at all: rewarded ads only play when you tap an ad button. You can play the whole game without watching any.

4. Children

Scoop Snake is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the ads, contact us and we will help you request deletion from Google.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

6. Contact

Questions or requests: aymanabdelsalam81@gmail.com
